# Python-Basics-Instacart-Project
Project based training introduction to Python
## Project Overview
This repository presents a comprehensive exploratory analysis of Instacart's sales data, aiming to identify significant customer purchasing trends. The analysis seeks to provide actionable insights for targeted marketing strategies. It addresses essential questions from the sales and marketing teams, such as identifying peak order days and times, understanding spending behaviors, and analyzing product popularity across different departments. Furthermore, the project investigates customer segmentation, looking into variations in behavior based on brand loyalty, region, age, income, and family status. These findings are supposed to assist Instacart in tailoring marketing campaigns to better engage various customer profiles, increase sales, and improve the effectiveness of promotions and advertisements.
## Key Questions
The sales team needs to know what the busiest days of the week and hours of the day are (i.e., the days and times with the most orders) in order to schedule ads at times when there are fewer orders.
 They also want to know whether there are particular times of the day when people spend the most money, as this might inform the type of products they advertise at these times. 
Instacart has a lot of products with different price tags. Marketing and sales want to use simpler price range groupings to help direct their efforts.
Are there certain types of products that are more popular than others? The marketing and sales teams want to know which departments have the highest frequency of product orders.
The marketing and sales teams are particularly interested in the different types of customers in their system and how their ordering behaviors differ. For example: 
What’s the distribution among users in regards to their brand loyalty (i.e., how often do they return to Instacart)? 
Are there differences in ordering habits based on a customer’s loyalty status?
Are there differences in ordering habits based on a customer’s region? 
Is there a connection between age and family status in terms of ordering habits? 
What different classifications does the demographic information suggest? Age? Income? Certain types of goods? Family status? 
What differences can you find in ordering habits of different customer profiles? Consider the price of orders, the frequency of orders, the products customers are ordering, and anything else you can think of.
